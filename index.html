Advanced HDPE Pipe Manufacturing Process

Our state-of-the-art extrusion technology ensures consistent quality, optimal material properties, and dimensional accuracy in every pipe we manufacture.

High-Grade Raw Material Selection

Vacuum sizing tanks ensure precise outer diameter while internal pressure maintains perfect roundness and wall thickness uniformity.

  • PE100 grade material
  • Optimal molecular weight distribution

Extrusion Process

High-performance extruders melt and homogenize PE100 compound under precisely controlled temperatures, ensuring consistent melt flow and molecular structure.

  • Single-screw extruder with barrier design
  • Precise temperature zone control

Cooling

Rapid and controlled cooling sets the final pipe dimensions and ensures uniform wall thickness throughout.

  • Water cooling tanks
  • Controlled cooling rate

Sizing

Precision sizing sleeves and vacuum tanks maintain exact outer diameter tolerances as specified.

  • Vacuum sizing tanks
  • Dimensional accuracy ±0.1mm

Quality Control

Every pipe undergoes rigorous testing including hydrostatic pressure, dimensional checks, and material validation before dispatch.

  • Online wall thickness gauging
  • 100% hydrostatic testing

Marking

Permanent inkjet marking includes pipe size, pressure rating, material grade, production date and standard compliance.

  • ISO compliant marking
  • Traceability codes

Cutting

Automated planetary cutters ensure clean, square cuts at precise lengths with no burring or deformation.

  • Automated cut-to-length
  • Burr-free finish

Packaging

Pipes are bundled, wrapped and labeled for secure transportation meeting customer and export specifications.

  • Custom bundle sizes
  • Export-grade packaging
